Each large sticker must:

- Measure exactly **7 × 4.6 cm** at final printed size.
- Include:
  - The child’s character image.
  - The child’s full name.
  - The school name.
  - The class, grade, or educational stage.
  - A circular HeroKid-style monogram containing the first letter of the child’s first name.

- Follow the branded visual style of reference PDF `(3)`.
- Use a different, coordinated color palette for each character.
- Keep all text readable and safely inside the cutting area.
- Avoid placing names, faces, or important elements too close to the sticker edges.
